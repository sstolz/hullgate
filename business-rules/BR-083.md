# BR-083 · An outbound dynamic registration is kept before use and never redone on its own

## Rule

The `client_id` and the secret obtained by dynamic registration (RFC 7591) are kept before any authorization, the `client_id` in clear and only the secret encrypted; they are redone only by an explicit command, because registering again orphans the grants.

## Clarifications

- Proved against the fake provider (P1 a). Source: spec l. 631-634; phase 2 matrix (Notion).
- `application reregister` first displays the connections that reference the application. In the same transaction as the write of the new `client_id` and the new secret, it sets them to `réauthentification requise` and erases their tokens; their revocation at the provider is attempted on a best-effort basis with the **old** client, outside the transaction. The operation is audited (BR-066 v2).
- The `client_id` is not a secret (RFC 6749 §2.2): in clear like that of a declared application (§3.1, §6.5, BR-081), it appears in the authorization URL and is displayed by `application list` and the export (BR-070 v2); the returned secret follows BR-087. The returned authentication method is kept with the application (`auth_method`).
- The registration asks for `token_endpoint_auth_method: none` (public PKCE client, decision B2); if the provider returns a secret anyway, it is kept encrypted and presented per the returned method. `client_secret_expires_at` is kept and displayed by `application list`; an expired secret gives `invalid_client`, classified in the BR-092 table. `registration_access_token` (RFC 7592) is not kept (§Out of Scope).
