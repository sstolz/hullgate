# BR-101 · The gate embeds no analysis tool, except coverage measurement

## Rule

The recipe of `make gate` calls neither `qa`, nor any `qa-*` target, nor `sonar`; its `tests` step writes `coverage.xml` (measurement of `src/`, relative paths) by running the same tests as before, and its failure conditions remain those of BR-035. A call to `qa`, to a `qa-*` target or to `sonar` in the gate's recipe is an error of the test `test_br_101_gate_offline.py`, hence of the gate.

## Clarifications

- Reason: the gate stays local, fast, deterministic; a vulnerability published tomorrow must not make yesterday's commit fail.
- The coverage measurement has no threshold in the gate (BR-102).
