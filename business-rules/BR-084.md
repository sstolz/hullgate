# BR-084 · Every outbound call to a provider is bounded

## Rule

Discovery, registration, code exchange, account reading (`userinfo`), renewal and revocation toward a provider use https, without redirect, with a hard deadline of 10 s on the whole exchange and 256 KiB of response; a non-public address is refused, except that a host that the service record declares as an internal host may resolve to a private address, never to loopback, link-local, the unspecified address or multicast; any gap is a failure of the call.

## Clarifications

- The exception is named by the operator when the service is declared (`--internal-host`), audited at that moment (BR-066 v2), and applies only to the **host and port** of the record's base URL, compared after normalization (lowercase, trailing dot removed, IDNA, canonical IP literal: `127.1`, `0x7f000001` and `[::ffff:127.0.0.1]` are read as `127.0.0.1`). `service declare` displays and audits the resolved addresses allowed at that moment. No environment variable or configuration option lifts the bound; the tests inject their own fetcher, as for BR-062.
- Like BR-062: **all** the resolved addresses of a name are verified (a mixed public and private resolution is refused outside the exception), and the connection is made to the verified address, the name serving for SNI and certificate verification; a DNS rebind between the verification and the connection therefore has no effect.
- Mechanism: the bounded fetcher of BR-062 (`cimd.py`: `http.client`, `socket`, `ssl`, worker thread, connection to the verified address), extended to POST and to headers (`hullgate.outbound`). The default TLS context loads OpenSSL's compiled paths, never `SSL_CERT_FILE` nor `SSL_CERT_DIR`. `httpx` is not used for outbound calls (it reads `HTTPS_PROXY`, `ALL_PROXY`, `SSL_CERT_FILE` and `.netrc` by default). No proxy or certificate environment variable is read. Learnings from F-005: the deadline is hard on the whole exchange (DNS and connection included), proved with a slow drip in keep-alive, `Connection: close` and HTTP/1.0.
