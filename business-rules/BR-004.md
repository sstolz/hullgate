# BR-004 · Every « échoué » or « non jouable » test is a documented gap

## Rule

The report contains exactly 16 test lines; each « échoué » or « non jouable » test carries a gap with its cause, its fix classed by the target's verdict criterion — "overlay" or "in-core" for ContextForge (BR-007), "library" or "in-house" for the Go POC (BR-023) — and its estimated cost in development days; an incomplete gap is a report structure error.

## Clarifications

- The class of a POC fix informs the weighing of the stack, it does not change the verdict (BR-023).
- "Library" or "in-house" applies to any stack POC (Go POC, Python POC), according to the building blocks of BR-023.
