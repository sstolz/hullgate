# BR-210 · A security act asks for the passkey again

## Rule

Adding or deleting a passkey and changing the allow-list require an assertion of at most 5 minutes old; without one the API answers 403 `fresh_auth_required` and the act does nothing. `POST /admin/api/v1/session/fresh` takes an assertion and writes `fresh_until`; session value and CSRF token do not change.

## Clarifications

- Deleting one's last passkey is refused: the account would have no way back but BR-228.
- The acts are named: this list is the rule's population. A later feature adding an act of the same family adds it here by a new version.
