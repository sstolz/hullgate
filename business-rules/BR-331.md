# BR-331 · No followed text asks to report on a ticket

## Rule

No guide, README, deployment file, code message or test fixture instructs its reader to post, paste or read anything on a ticket; a step's outcome is its expected result; a line matching a reporting idiom fails the test.

## Clarifications
- Specs, extracted rules and trial reports keep their reporting sentences as history; BR-042 v2 removes its own.
