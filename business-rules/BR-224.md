# BR-224 · An import deletes only what it was told to, and identifies by path

## Rule

Nodes, placements and connections are matched by path and name; what exists and is missing from the document is a deletion, listed and applied only when the apply request says `apply_deletions: true`; a segment change is a deletion plus a creation and is never inferred as a rename.

## Clarifications

- A rename inferred from similarity could keep or cut lineages by a guess (BR-046).
- A setting override missing from the document is a change (back to inherited), not a deletion.
