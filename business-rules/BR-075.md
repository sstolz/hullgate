# BR-075 · A removed connection never comes back to life, nor do its tokens

## Rule

Removing a connection erases its tokens and its placements in the same transaction and reserves its id; a connection recreated under the same name receives none of the previous one's tokens.

## Clarifications

- Same mechanism as BR-045 for nodes. Deleting a role removes its placements in the transaction of BR-045 (spec line 1122).
- Revocation at the provider is attempted on a best-effort basis; its failure is audited (BR-066 v2) without preventing the removal.
- `remove` takes the renewal lease of BR-089 like `revoke` (BR-095): it waits for the end of a renewal in progress, reads the **current** tokens, erases and increments `generation` in the same transaction, then revokes at the provider outside the transaction.
- `remove` is refused as long as a placement of the connection is under a locked node, and the refusal names the lock (BR-049, parity with the deletion of an ancestor of a locked node).
- Without a key (BR-088), `remove` erases locally and skips the revocation at the provider, audited as "key_unavailable".
- The prefix of a removed connection is freed (its placements are removed with it; a new placement is audited); only its id is reserved.
