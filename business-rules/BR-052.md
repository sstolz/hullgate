# BR-052 · Token durations are fixed at issuance and capped in sensitive environments

## Rule

An OAuth access token lives 1 hour; an OAuth renewal lineage lives the effective value of `token_duration` on its node (default 30 days), and an operator token the duration requested at issuance or, failing that, that same value, both capped at 7 days if that node is sensitive or under a sensitive environment; the expiry is computed at issuance and does not change if the setting changes afterwards, and a token or renewal presented after its expiry is refused (401 `invalid_token` at the access point, `invalid_grant` at `/token`).

## Clarifications

- Spec lines 410 and 425: access token 1 h; renewal 30 days with rotation, 7 in a sensitive environment. Spec line 315: token duration is an inherited setting. Spec line 446: `--duration` at issuance of an operator token, which takes precedence over the setting, always under the sensitive cap.
- The renewal lineage expires at a fixed date from the authorization: rotation does not extend the lineage.
- The authorization code lives 120 s; a request pending at the authorization barrier, 10 min.
- The 7-day cap is evaluated at issuance: marking an environment sensitive neither shortens nor revokes any existing token (BR-047 displays and audits the count); sensitivity is not a setting, but the duration fixed at issuance does not change either. Operator decision of 2026-10-28 (S3).
