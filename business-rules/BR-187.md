# BR-187 · A new tool on a placement pinned whole is suspended

## Rule

On a placement that already holds baselines and is pinned whole, a tool without a baseline is suspended as new, as BR-181 suspends a changed one.

## Clarifications

- "Holds baselines" is any baseline on the placement: turning a placement that holds tool pins only into a whole pin suspends, at its next listing, each pinned tool without a baseline; `tool diff` shows them `suspended` and one `tool validate <node> <connection>` lifts them.
