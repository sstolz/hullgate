# BR-078 · A sharing outside the client or between sensitive and non-sensitive requires a traced confirmation

## Rule

Placing a connection on a role of another client than its original client, or on a role whose effective sensitivity (BR-047) differs from that of a role where it is already placed, is refused (code 2, API 409 `share_confirmation_required`) without `--confirm-share` or the API's equivalent; with it, the warning and the confirmation are written to the audit and the confirmation is kept on the placement. The console shows the warning, recommends a dedicated connection, and requires an unticked-by-default checkbox.

## Clarifications

- A single mechanism for the specification's safeguard (lines 147-148, 212) and the operator's decision of 2026-10-28 on prod / non-prod.
- The audit entry names the connection, the two roles and their two sensitivities.

## Examples

### Example 1

- **Given** `mpx/gitea-dev` is placed on `mpx/cairn/dev/agent` (non-sensitive)
- **When** the operator places it on `mpx/cairn/prod/review` (sensitive) without the flag
- **Then** refusal code 2 "sharing between a sensitive and a non-sensitive role: add --confirm-share", nothing written; with the flag, the placement and the confirmation audit entry are written.
