# BR-068 · No administration route without authentication

## Rule

No listener serves a route that reads or modifies the tree, the settings, the tokens, the clients or the audit without authenticating the operator; in F-005, the administration listener serves only `/healthz`, the public listener serves only its probes, the routes under `/o/<id>/` (access points, authorization servers) and the `/.well-known/…` metadata, and management goes through the CLI in the container.

## Clarifications

- Reason: port 4791 answers to the neighboring containers of the production server's Traefik network (F-004 review). Authentication of the web administration arrives with HULL-11 (passkey).
- No test route (signing key, simulated failure) exists in the image: the bench injects its failures from outside (BR-071).
- F-014: the public routes are organisation-scoped, under `/o/<id>/` with the instance's identifier (BR-161); any other path, the forms in force before F-014 included, answers 404. F-016's v2 of this rule (the admin listener's console and API) is not extracted yet: its closure carries this text forward (F-016 §4.1).
