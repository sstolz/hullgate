# BR-130 · Without a policy, a connection placed on at least one sensitive role has no tool, on all its roles

## Rule

As long as #10 has not provided a policy, no tool of a connection placed on at least one sensitive role (sensitive environment, BR-047) is announced or called, on any of the roles that carry it; the sensitivity of the connection is the maximum of that of its placements, re-read from the database on every request.

## Clarifications

- A safety guard and not a mode: it disappears when #10 replaces it, and BR-130 is then modified (Q4, H4).
- Cause: BR-078 allows, with confirmation, a connection placed on both a sensitive role and a non-sensitive role, and BR-079 does not prevent a later marking from creating this sharing. Closing only the sensitive role would let the non-sensitive role serve, with the sensitive connection's token, the writes that the brief's guard closes.
- A new placement on a sensitive role or the sensitive marking of a placed role takes effect on the next request, warm cache included (BR-112); the list of a non-sensitive role then omits the connection (BR-111, reason "sensitive").
- The guard is decided at stage 1 of BR-113, which alone carries the proof by absence (0 `get_access`, 0 exchange with the upstream; a renewal is a provider call, in the upstream log per BR-002): it requires no upstream definition.
- `connection status` says "tools closed: placed on a sensitive role"; `connection tools` refuses such a connection (§3.2).
