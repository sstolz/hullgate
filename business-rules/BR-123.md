# BR-123 · The relay consults a policy before announcing and before calling

## Rule

For each tool at the announcement and at each call, the relay asks the policy with the role, the connection, the upstream name and definition (and the arguments at the call) and neither announces nor calls what it hides or refuses; a policy that raises an error refuses every tool concerned (fail closed).

## Clarifications

- The policy is the action policy of F-015 (`hullgate.policy`), the relay's default: a role's policy state is read from the database once per request (BR-112) and the same evaluator decides `tools/list`, `tools/call` and `policy preview` (BR-174).
- The policy receives the upstream definition: it is consulted only at stage 2 of BR-113, never before the relay has obtained a token and, at most, a list.
- The reason of a refusal goes to the audit (BR-185); an exception is the reason `policy_error`.
