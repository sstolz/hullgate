# BR-108 · Concurrency toward the upstream is bounded and separate from OAuth renewal

## Rule

The relay runs at most 4 simultaneous exchanges per connection and 32 in total, in a thread pool separate from the outbound OAuth client's; beyond that, the call is refused at once (`upstream_busy`, BR-116) with no queue and no exchange.

## Clarifications

- Cause: a relay with a 60 s deadline would saturate in under a minute the 16 slots of a shared pool, including those of token renewal.
- A call refused for saturation does not count as an upstream outage (no "not before" date, BR-124) and is audited with the outcome `upstream_busy` (BR-122), not as an upstream outage.
