# BR-343 · A ticket leaves the private tracker selected, screened and labelled

## Rule

A private ticket becomes a GitHub issue only if it is on the list the operator approved, its title and body pass the denylist of BR-325, the scanner of BR-326 and the former-name patterns, its body opens on its migration line naming its `HULL-NN`, and it carries only labels of §3.7 with exactly one `area/*`; its comments are not carried. A new issue written from a private ticket's text passes the same screening and label check. A ticket failing one condition is not created.
