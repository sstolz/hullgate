# BR-229 · The setup guide reads the state, and has no acts of its own

## Rule

The guide's steps are computed from the instance's state (a client, a service, a connection, a placement, an issued credential exist); each step links to the ordinary screen where its act is done, and following the link inside the console opens or focuses that screen's own control for the act, without reloading the console; the guide writes nothing but its own dismissal. A step that disagrees with the state, or a link that does not land on its control, is signalled by the test.

## Clarifications

- The five targets are distinct and served by the server (BR-211); the table of F-023 §3.1 is the population.
- A control the state cannot offer (placement with no connection) gives way to the act that must come first, focused (F-023 §3.1).
- Landing writes nothing (BR-234). The guide renders no form: the opened dialog or field is the screen's own.
- v1 → v2: "each links to the ordinary screen" is completed by where the link lands and how (HULL-109, finding F1 of HULL-11's acceptance).
