# BR-181 · A pinned tool whose definition changes is suspended

## Rule

A pinned tool whose upstream definition no longer hashes to its baseline is suspended on that placement: hidden and refused, even if the upstream reverts.

## Clarifications

- The first listing under a pin records the SHA-256 of the tool's upstream definition as its baseline.
- `tool validate` alone lifts a suspension (BR-182).
- Hashed: `title`, `description`, `inputSchema`, `outputSchema`, `annotations`, in canonical JSON, on the upstream definition, never the rewritten one (specification §Paramètre account). A renamed tool is a removal and a new tool.
- The first baseline of a placement trusts what it reads: a definition changed before the pin is not detected (§12).
- Detection happens when a listing is filled, so at most 300 s after the change (BR-112).
- Suspension does not lift by itself, even when the upstream reverts: a tool that flips back and forth is the case to look at.
- A suspension, of a changed tool or of a new one (BR-187), is a `pinned_definition` row with `suspended_at` set, written in its own transaction whatever becomes of the audit entry, and a failure of that write fails the listing closed; this column is the state of record for the evaluator, `tool diff` and #12.
- Each change also writes one `tool.suspended` audit entry per (placement, tool, hash), a trace and never the state; #12's alert reads `suspended_at` (F-019).
- A baseline stays while the pin is lifted and is compared again if the pin returns: the direction of the surprise is a suspension.
- A tool that disappears shows as `removed` in `tool diff`; validating drops its baseline.
- Detection runs at every listing of a placement, in a transaction of its own: the relay's fill and the CLI listings `connection tools`, `policy preview` and `policy arg`; the relay reads the role's state after the listings of its request, so a suspension just written already hides the tool.
- Lifting a pin (`policy pin off`, `unpin-tool`, removing an argument rule) does not lift a suspension: `tool validate` alone does.
- A pinning state that cannot be read is `policy_error` (BR-185); a database that refuses the write is an outage. Both fail closed. Upstream text UTF-8 cannot encode fails the relay's listing closed the same way.
