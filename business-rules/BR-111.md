# BR-111 · An upstream outage does not erase the tools of the role's other connections

## Rule

When the upstream of a connection does not answer `tools/list` (deadline, cut, 5xx, invalid or oversized response, pagination beyond the ceilings), the list returns the tools of the role's other connections, omits this connection and audits the omission with its reason; it is never replaced by the tools of a stale cache, and a call to a tool of this connection fails with `upstream_unavailable`.

## Clarifications

- A list entirely empty because every upstream is down is the same empty list as a role without connection; the difference is read in the audit.
- A cache entry younger than 300 s is served without contacting the upstream (BR-112); beyond that, an upstream outage omits the connection: there is no fallback cache.
- The reasons audited at an omission are distinct: `outage`, `deadline` (BR-109), `version` (BR-118), `session` (BR-107), `policy_error` (BR-185: the policy evaluator raised for this connection), `invalid_name` and `too_many_names` (BR-110), `pagination` (BR-109).
