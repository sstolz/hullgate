# BR-023 · POC "go" criterion

## Rule

The verdict is "go" if the isolation lines (T02 to T09, T12, T13, T14) are « réussi » on the delivered POC, and if the sum of the estimated costs of the remaining gaps does not exceed 5 days; "no-go" otherwise; no verdict as long as a line is « non joué ».

## Clarifications

- Isolation line: a test whose failure lets a token reach another role, an account or a tool outside its scope. In the POC, these functions are code that we write: a failure not fixed within the budget is evidence against the stack, not a gap to cost.
- Remaining gap: a test outside isolation (T01, T10, T11, T15, T16) or a hypothesis line (Gitea relay, generations, building blocks) not met, with its costed fix. Threshold on the **sum**, not per gap: ten gaps of 4 days do not make a go. 5 days = the upper bound of the POC estimate ("one more POC"), a recommended value that the operator adjusts at the freeze.
- Class of a fix (BR-004): "library" = obtained by the chosen building block or its configuration — Go POC: `fosite`, `go-sdk`, `x/oauth2`, `modernc.org/sqlite`; Python POC: `mcp` (server, client, `mcp.server.auth`), `authlib`, `PyJWT`, `sqlite3`; "in-house" = POC code in place of a building block. The class informs the weighing of the stack, it does not change the verdict.
- In-house OAuth plan B (H4) done and played by the acceptance run: not a gap of the verdict; the "maturity of the OAuth building block" line is "not met" with the measured cost of plan B.
- Python POC: line H-10 (checks outside the acceptance run, BR-025) not met = remaining gap, counted in the sum; line H-9 (core requirements, BR-026) not met = costed gap in the comparison, **outside the sum**, as for the Go POC (operator decision at the F-003 freeze, 2026-10-28). Neither is an isolation line.
- The "Claude.ai and iOS" line is "awaiting operator" (BR-020) and counts neither for nor against; a "go" is worded "go, Claude.ai / iOS reservation open" as long as it is.
- A "go" with gaps lists the cost of each; weighing Go against another language remains with the operator (BR-008 v2).
