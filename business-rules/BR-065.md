# BR-065 · Nothing is issued without being recorded

## Rule

Every issuance (code, access token, renewal, operator token) and every revocation is recorded with its audit entry in a single write transaction, and nothing is returned to the requester if it fails.

## Clarifications

- `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction (R-06), bounded wait time; failure → BR-064.
