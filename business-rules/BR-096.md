# BR-096 · A reauthorization does not silently change account

## Rule

When the code exchange of a reauthorization returns an account different from the recorded account, or when one of the two is unknown, `connection authorize` is refused (code 2) without `--confirm-account`: no token is written and the old ones stay in place; with this flag, the audit carries the account and the scopes before and after.

## Clarifications

- Source: spec l. 1127-1128 ("no silent account change"); the operator refuses any AI token on their personal account (learning `inbox/2026-10-27-product.md`). Same form as `--confirm-share` (BR-078).
- The tokens obtained by the refused exchange are revoked on a best-effort basis, never written; the `state` being consumed (BR-082), the operator reruns `reauthorize` then `authorize --confirm-account`.
- The account is read through the model's path (§3.1, BR-080). A provided token is not reauthorized (BR-085): the rule does not target it.

## Examples

### Example 1

- **Given** `mpx/gitea-dev` is `active` under the account `cairn-ai`
- **When** the operator reauthorizes it and signs in at Gitea as `alice`
- **Then** `authorize` answers code 2 "different account: cairn-ai → alice; add --confirm-account", the connection stays `active` under `cairn-ai` with its tokens.
