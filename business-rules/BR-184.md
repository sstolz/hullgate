# BR-184 · The role's response cap replaces a larger result

## Rule

A `tools/call` result whose size exceeds the effective `response_cap` of the role is not truncated: it is replaced by `response_too_large`, and the call is not repeated.

## Clarifications

- Size: the compact JSON of the upstream result, the figure audited as `response_bytes`.
- The cap is at most the 1 MiB of BR-107: the setting refuses more.
- The tool has run: the fixed text of the error says the result was withheld, not that the call failed. The client decides (BR-117).
- The cap covers tool results, not `tools/list`.
- The replacement is a gateway error result whose fixed text starts with the code `response_too_large` and says the tool ran and its result was withheld; the `relay.tool_call` entry carries issue `response_too_large`, the real `response_bytes` and the effective `response_cap` in bytes.
- The cap is the role's effective setting (BR-048), read with the role's state at each call (BR-112).
