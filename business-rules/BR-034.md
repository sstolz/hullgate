# BR-034 · The administration has its own port, and is routed only by BR-230

## Rule

The administration listens on a port distinct from the public listener; on the production server that port is published on `127.0.0.1` only, and no Traefik router reaches it unless BR-230's route is enabled.

## Clarifications

- With `ADMIN_HOST` empty, administration stays behind the SSH tunnel; BR-230 supplies the optional public route.
- Operator access: SSH tunnel to `127.0.0.1:${ADMIN_LOCAL_PORT}` (runbook).
