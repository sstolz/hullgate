# BR-066 · The audit traces issuances, revocations, refusals and tree changes, without any secret

## Rule

The gateway writes an audit entry for every issuance, revocation, refusal at the access point or at the authorization server, barrier failure, registration or removal of a client (CLI or RFC 7591), change of the tree or of a setting, and for every event of the connection library — creation, authorization, reauthorization (with account and scopes before and after, BR-096), replacement of the value of a provided token (BR-085), revocation and removal of a connection; declaration and rediscovery of a service (endpoints, and internal-host exception with the admitted addresses where applicable); declaration and re-registration of an application; placement and removal of placement; sharing confirmation (BR-078); outcome of a renewal (renewed, permanent, transient, uncertain, anomaly); refusal reported by HULL-9; refusal of `authorize`; key unavailable or different; integrity error; declaration and removal of the MCP upstream of a service (BR-106); diagnosis, name purge and invalid tool name of a connection (BR-110); every tool call, every relay refusal and every omission of a connection from `tools/list` (BR-122); "not before" date of a report (BR-128); upstream requests refused to the client (BR-121); vocabulary migration (`instance.vocabulary_migrated`, actor `migration`); administrator created, activated; enrolment link issued, used, refused; sign-in success, failure and limit; sign-out; passkey added or deleted; allow-list change; undo, with the entry undone; import applied, with its identifier; audit export —, with UTC timestamp, actor, node (path and identifier), action, result, reason and, for a change, values before and after (spec lines 334-335); no entry contains a token value, a code, a PKCE verifier, a secret or a return URL, nor an argument or a result of a tool call (BR-122), nor a full fingerprint, nor the body or the `error_description` of a provider response (only the `error` code from the closed list of BR-092, truncated).

Actor form `admin:<id>` beside `operator-cli`. The prohibition on secret values covers enrolment links and session values.

The events also include `consent.approved`, `access.body_refused` and `rate_limited.refused`; as the exception to one entry per refusal, a refusal under BR-303, BR-304, BR-306 or BR-307, and an unknown credential beyond BR-308's budget, are audited as BR-312 says.

## Clarifications

- Actor: `operator-cli`, the OAuth `client_id`, or the identifier of an operator token (never its value). Registering or removing a client: actor `operator-cli` (CLI) or `anonymous` (dynamic registration, RFC 7591), `after.client_id` at registration and `before.client_id` at removal, never a secret.
- Append-only table: no command or migration modifies or deletes an entry (retention: HULL-12).
- A refusal is served even if its audit entry could not be written (the failure is logged on the error output); an issuance is not (BR-065). This is the explicit exception to BR-064, which exempts that one write only.
- Tool calls are audited since F-008 (BR-122). The confirmation of a shared connection (BR-078) has been written since F-006.
- Library actions (F-006): `service.declare`, `service.rediscover`, `service.remove`, `application.declare`, `application.register`, `application.reregister`, `application.remove`, `connection.add`, `connection.authorize_open`, `connection.authorize`, `connection.authorize_refused`, `connection.replace_value`, `connection.prefix`, `connection.place`, `connection.unplace`, `connection.share_confirmed`, `connection.share_signal`, `connection.renew`, `connection.refusal_signal`, `connection.revoke`, `connection.upstream_revoke`, `connection.remove`, `connection.key_unavailable`, `connection.integrity_error`, `vault.key_check_forgotten`. Actor `operator-cli` for the CLI, `gateway` for `get_access`, the renewal and `signal_refusal`.
- `connection.key_unavailable` is written once per (connection, reason) as long as the reason does not change (review 417, m2).
- Relay and upstream actions (F-008): `service.upstream`, `service.upstream_remove`, `connection.tools_list`, `connection.tools_purge`, `connection.tool_name_invalid`, `connection.tools_refused`, `relay.tool_call`, `relay.list_omitted`, `relay.upstream_request_refused`, `relay.signal_pause`. Actor `operator-cli` for the CLI; for the relay, the identifier of the operator token or the `client_id` of the caller (BR-122).
- A vocabulary migration does not rewrite history: the rows written before it keep their French values, and the marker row says where the vocabulary of the values changes. The marker is written only if the table contained at least one row.
- Actions of the action policy (F-015, BR-188): `policy.mode`, `policy.list_add`, `policy.list_remove`, `policy.argument_set`, `policy.argument_remove`, `policy.pin`, `tool.classify`, `tool.accept`, `tool.discovered`, `tool.suspended`, `tool.validate`, with before and after; a policy refusal carries its reason in the `reason` column of `relay.tool_call` (BR-185). `tool.suspended` is written by the actor `gateway`, the others by `operator-cli`.

**Rule** — The gateway writes an audit entry for every issuance, revocation, refusal at the access point or at the authorization server, barrier failure, registration or removal of a client (CLI or RFC 7591) and change of the tree or of a setting, with UTC timestamp, actor, node (path and identifier), action, result, reason and, for a change, values before and after (spec lines 334-335); no entry contains a token value, a code, a PKCE verifier, a secret or a full fingerprint.
