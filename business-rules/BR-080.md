# BR-080 · `connexion etat` says what the connection is and where it serves, without any secret

## Rule

`hullgate connection status` shows for each connection its service, its account, the granted scopes, its state, its deadline and its placements with the effective sensitivity of each role, and never a token, secret or code value; `--json` returns the same fields.

## Clarifications

- Source: spec line 147 ("where used" view), 175-177. No other `connection`, `application` or `service` command prints a secret value.
- The account is read at the code exchange through the path that the service's model fixes (§3.1: `userinfo` endpoint, call bounded by BR-084), kept in clear (it is not a secret) and never re-read at renewal. For a provided token: the `--account` label entered at `ajouter`, otherwise "unknown".
- `status` also shows the "estimated" deadline (BR-091), the last outcome `uncertain` or `anomaly` (BR-090, BR-092) and, without a key, `key_absent`, `key_invalid` or `key_different` (BR-088).
- `status` without a name ends with the line `connections key: usable` or the reason for the unavailability, readable even without any connection: it is the reading the runbook does after each deployment (§12, forgotten key).
