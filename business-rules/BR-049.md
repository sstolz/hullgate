# BR-049 · A node's lock freezes its subtree; only that node unlocks

## Rule

While a node is locked, no change of structure (add, rename, move, delete), of setting or of sensitivity is accepted on it or on one of its descendants, and no change of the path of one of its ancestors (rename, move) nor any deletion of an ancestor is accepted, since it would change or make disappear the locked node; the refusal names the node that locked, and only that node can be unlocked.

## Clarifications

- Guard of locked descendants: deleting (BR-045), renaming or moving (BR-046, amendment of 2026-10-28) an ancestor of a locked node is refused. Deletion already is (batch A); rename and move are delivered in batch B. Tests: `test_br_046_rename.py` (case "locked descendant") and `test_br_049_freeze.py`.
- Origin: validated prototype HULL-18 ("frozen"). The specification lists "pinning" among the inherited settings (spec line 315) in the sense of **pinned tool definitions** (spec lines 415, 844, 952-953), which belong to HULL-10. The two notions are distinct: the core names its own "lock" and HULL-10 will declare "pinning" on BR-048's engine.
- Locking is `settings set <chemin> lock true` (or `tree lock`); unlocking a node locked by an ancestor is refused, naming the ancestor.
- Compatibility with BR-048: no `lock=false` override exists below the organisation (it would mask the lock of an ancestor placed afterwards, the nearest override winning); unlocking a node therefore removes its override instead of writing `false`, and only the organisation carries a `false` value.
- **Outside the lock** (H11): issuing or revoking an operator token, authorizing an OAuth client, adding or removing a client (`client add` / `remove`) or registering it dynamically (RFC 7591), revoking a lineage. The lock protects the tree and its settings, not access; admitting a client is a matter of access (operator decision of 2026-10-28, S5), and a revocation must always remain possible.
- Placements (HULL-8) and policy settings (HULL-10) will be subject to the same lock.
- F-006 (hypothesis H2: the placement is a role setting, spec line 155): placing a connection on a role or removing that placement is refused under a locked node; `connection remove` is refused as long as a placement of the connection is under a locked node (parity with the deletion of an ancestor, BR-045); `connection revoke` is outside the lock (a revocation always remains possible); the refusal names the locking node.
- F-015: every `policy` write (`mode`, `add`, `remove`, `arg`, `arg-remove`, `pin`, `pin-tool`, `unpin-tool`) and `tool validate` is refused on a locked node, before any upstream exchange; a `tool classify` or `clear` that lowers a tool's class is refused while a placement of the service lies under a locked node, a raising passes (BR-172, D10); the refusal names the lock.
