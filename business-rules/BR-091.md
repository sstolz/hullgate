# BR-091 · A token that expires in less than 60 s is never returned

## Rule

Requesting the token of a connection whose deadline is in less than 60 s first triggers its renewal; the token returned is valid for at least 60 s, otherwise the call fails with a typed error.

## Clarifications

- D1 a: renewal on use, no background task. Clock injected in the tests.
- **Incomplete token response**: without `expires_in`, the model's default deadline (§3.1, 3600 s) applies, displayed as "estimated"; `expires_in` ≤ 60 makes the rule untenable: the code exchange is refused (code 2) and a renewal is a definitive outcome, reason `expiry_too_short` (BR-092); without `refresh_token`, the connection goes to `réauthentification requise` when its token enters the 60 s margin, without a call to the provider; a renewal without a new `refresh_token` keeps the old one; different scopes granted at renewal are recorded and audited before and after.
