# BR-074 · A connection is named `<client>/<nom>` and carries its originating client

## Rule

A connection belongs to the organisation, is named `<client>/<nom>` (existing client, segment-shaped name, unique under that client) and references a declared service; a nonexistent client or service, or a name already taken, is refused without writing anything.

## Clarifications

- The client is referenced by its node id: renaming its segment changes the display, not the connection. Deleting a client that carries connections is refused (clarification of BR-045).
- A service is a declarative record (model, base URL, requested scopes, discovered endpoints, BR-097): `gitea` and `generic` are provided, what each one fixes is in §3.1; the operator declares the servers (`service declare`). Source: spec lines 139, 212, 625.
