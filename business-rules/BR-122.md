# BR-122 · Every tool call and every relay refusal is audited, without argument or result

## Rule

For each tool call and each omission of a connection at `tools/list`, the gateway writes an audit entry carrying the actor (operator token identifier or `client_id`), the role, the connection, the public name and the upstream name, the version negotiated with the upstream, the outcome (closed list: `ok`, `tool_error`, `refused`, `upstream_unavailable`, `upstream_busy`, `deadline_exceeded`, `uncertain_outcome`, `response_too_large`, `request_too_large`, `connection_reauth_required`, `cancelled`, `internal_error`), the duration and the sizes; it never writes an argument, a result, a token or a header.

A `tools/call` refused under BR-303 or BR-304 is audited as BR-312 says, with no `relay.tool_call` entry; the closed issue list is unchanged.

## Clarifications

- For "unknown tool" (outcome `refused`), the connection and the upstream name do not exist: the entry carries only the known fields, and the public name, a hostile client value, is truncated to 64 characters and filtered on `[A-Za-z0-9_-]` before being written.
- One entry at the end of the call; if its writing fails, the result is served all the same and the failure is logged on the error output, as for a refusal (BR-066) (H9).
- Optional fields, read from the code. Entry of a call (`relay.tool_call`): `connection` and `upstream_name` (absent when no connection is known, "unknown tool"), `version` (absent when no upstream was reached), `stream: true` (response served as a stream, BR-120) and the `reason` column (reason for a failure on the upstream side, for example "session"; absent from the outcome `ok`). Entry of a list omission (`relay.list_omitted`): `reason` carries BR-111's reason, the detail the role, the connection, the outcome and the duration. Entry of a reporting "not before" date (`relay.signal_pause`, BR-128): `pause_s`. Entry of an exchange in which the upstream addressed requests to the client (`relay.upstream_request_refused`, BR-121): `method` (of the first) and `count`. None carries an argument, a result, a token or a header.
- BR-066's final sweep of the test databases is extended: no argument or result value of the fake upstream in any database.
- The audit grows by one line per call; its retention belongs to #12 (§12).
- F-015: a `relay.tool_call` refused by the policy carries in `reason` one value of the closed list `suspended`, `denied`, `not_allowlisted`, `write_closed`, `above_mode`, `argument_value`, `policy_error` (BR-185); `argument_value` names no value. `relay.list_omitted` gains the reason `policy_error` and loses `sensitive` (BR-111).
- F-015: a result replaced by the role's response cap (BR-184) is audited with issue `response_too_large`, its real `response_bytes` and the effective `response_cap` in bytes.
