# BR-093 · A transient failure changes neither the state nor the token

## Rule

A response that the BR-092 table classifies as transient or uncertain (network error, deadline exceeded, 5xx or 429 response, anomaly) during a renewal leaves the connection's state and encrypted token unchanged, and the caller receives a typed `access_temporarily_unavailable` error.

## Clarifications

- Rules out the fault of ruining a healthy connection over a passing outage (framing D2).
