# BR-079 · A tree change that creates a sharing is signaled

## Rule

Marking an environment sensitive, unmarking it, or moving a node in a way that makes a connection shared in the sense of BR-078 is signaled by the CLI before acting and in the audit, without blocking.

## Clarifications

- Decision E1 b. A block would touch BR-047 and BR-049: out of scope (§2).
- The console signals it too, in BR-214's dialog, before acting. That dialog and SCR-07.share also say that the neighbours of a shared connection share its call limit (F-020 BR-304).
