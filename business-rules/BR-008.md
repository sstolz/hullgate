# BR-008 · A trial or a stack-choice POC does not decide the stack

## Rule

A trial or a stack-choice POC (the ContextForge trial, the Go POC) writes neither `project.stack` nor a stack choice (neither an ADR nor a stack decision in the repository); it produces a verdict and a recommendation; the decision belongs to the operator. A violation makes `check_scope.py` fail and the PR is not merged.

## Clarifications

- Operator ruling at the F-002 freeze (2026-10-27): no new rule for the POC; the number BR-019, allocated to the draft, is never reassigned.
