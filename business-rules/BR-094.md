# BR-094 · `Retry-After` is honored, otherwise a bounded exponential fallback

## Rule

After a 429 or a transient or uncertain outcome of a renewal, no attempt takes place before a "not before" date kept in the database: `Retry-After` when the provider gives a valid one, capped at 15 min, otherwise a fallback of 1 s doubling at each failure up to 15 min; the date is shared by all processes, and the fallback returns to zero after a successful renewal or a successful `authorize`.

## Clarifications

- Source: spec l. 636. Scope: the renewal point (N1 a); tool calls fall under HULL-9.
- `Retry-After` in seconds or as an HTTP date; an invalid value or a past date counts as an absent header (fallback).
