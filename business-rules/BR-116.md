# BR-116 · The gateway's errors come from a closed list, with no token, URL or upstream body

## Rule

Every failure produced by the gateway or by the transport (handshake, HTTP, JSON-RPC, deadline, size, saturation, connection to reauthorize) is returned to the client as an `isError: true` result whose code is in the closed list `upstream_unavailable`, `upstream_busy` (with optional delay), `deadline_exceeded`, `uncertain_outcome`, `response_too_large`, `request_too_large`, `connection_reauth_required`, `internal_error`, `rate_limited` (with its delay), and whose text repeats neither a token, nor the upstream's URL, nor a body, nor an `error_description` of the upstream.

## Clarifications

- "Never a token or upstream URL in error messages" (HULL-1, l. 504) holds for what the gateway itself writes (H11); the content of a tool result falls under BR-126.
- `internal_error`: any unexpected exception of the gateway, without detail.
- A policy refusal is not in the list: it is "unknown tool" (BR-113, BR-123).
- Three phases, three codes: request not fully sent (resolution, connection, TLS, interrupted send; BR-093: transient) → `upstream_unavailable`; request fully sent and the **deadline** (15 s or 60 s, BR-107) reached without a whole response → `deadline_exceeded`; request fully sent and a **cut** or interrupted stream before the deadline → `uncertain_outcome`. The client acts the same for the last two (it does not know whether the tool acted); the distinction serves diagnosis.
- An upstream HTTP status other than 200, 401 and 429 (403, 404 outside a session, 5xx, unfollowed 3xx) is an `upstream_unavailable`; a 404 "unknown session" falls under BR-119.
