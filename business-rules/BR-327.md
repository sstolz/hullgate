# BR-327 · History candidates go to the operator, masked

## Rule

Every secret-scanner candidate in a commit reachable from any ref of a mirror clone of the private source's forge, pull-request refs included, is reported by commit, path, line, detector and value fingerprint, without its value; each distinct fingerprint receives the operator's verdict (`not a credential`, `not live` or `rotated`), a fingerprint equal to a tracked allowlist entry's inheriting that entry's reason; without a verdict on each distinct fingerprint, the ticket is not accepted.

## Clarifications
- The session never displays, copies, tests or rotates a candidate (guardrails §2 point 6).
- The report is `.cairn/runs/acts/HULL-98-history-scan.md`, the verdicts `.cairn/runs/acts/HULL-98-history-verdicts.md` (§3.10), both workshop files.
- The public repository is new (HULL-80): the private history is not published, the verdict guards the copies it lives in (the private source, its mirror).
