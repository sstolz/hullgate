# BR-215 · A change applies at the next request, and the console says when clients see it

## Rule

A change made from the console binds the next authorisation and the next `tools/list` of any client with no restart; the gateway pushes no `notifications/tools/list_changed`, and after a change affecting a role's tools the console states that connected clients see the new list at their next `tools/list`; a change that needs a restart fails the test.

## Clarifications

- Authorisation is already read from the database at each request (BR-112, BR-113), which holds whatever process wrote it.
- Q1, ticket text departed from: §9.
