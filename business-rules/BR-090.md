# BR-090 · The new token is recorded before it is returned

## Rule

A token resulting from a renewal is returned to the relay only after its encrypted write; if the write fails, nothing is returned and the connection's state is unchanged.

## Clarifications

- A lost single-use renewal orphans the grant (matrix, risk 2). Neighbor of BR-065 (inbound issuances).
- The renewed tokens and the audit entry of the outcome are written in the same transaction; if it fails, nothing is returned (parity with BR-065).
- A write that fails is retried as long as the caller holds the lease (BR-089); the tokens are kept only in memory during these attempts, never elsewhere.
- **`uncertain` outcome**: the renewal request has gone out and no response was written (deadline or cut after sending, write impossible until the end of the lease). The state and the encrypted token remain those from before; the outcome is audited (at the latest by the next caller, BR-089) and `status` displays it; it counts as a transient failure for BR-094. An `invalid_grant` that follows an uncertain outcome is audited with that reason. The replay that follows may destroy the grant at a provider with no grace: accepted risk, §12.
