# BR-330 · A fixture names nothing real

## Rule

A tracked fixture names nothing real: its hosts and e-mail domains are reserved names or public services the test exercises by name, its globally routable IP literals carry a comment saying they are mocked, and no answer of a real lookup is copied into it; a host or domain outside the reserved names and the pinned list fails the test, and an uncommented routable literal or a copied answer is a blocking review finding.

## Clarifications
- Reserved: `.test`, `.invalid`, `.example`, `example.com`, `example.org`, `example.net`, `localhost`; documentation ranges RFC 5737 and RFC 3849.
- Replacing an address keeps the property the test asserts (order, deduplication, class).
