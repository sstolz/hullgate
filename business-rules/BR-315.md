# BR-315 · The smoke proves the client address through Traefik

## Rule

`make prod-smoke` fails unless, through Traefik and for clients outside `TRUSTED_PROXIES`, the audited address is the client's, a forged `X-Forwarded-For` entry does not change it, and two clients are counted apart.
