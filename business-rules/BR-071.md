# BR-071 · The isolation bench judges v1

## Rule

The isolation bench has a `v1` target that builds the image from the root `Dockerfile` and plays a full pass: the checks outside the OAuth acceptance run (`H-2`), the core requirements (N1, N2, N4, N13 and the new `sensible` check of BR-051) and the N3 and nominal revocation harnesses all succeed, and none of the 16 tests transposed to the role (BR-012) is `échoué`; a test is « non jouable » only if one of its steps calls a relayed tool, evidence cited (BR-009).

## Clarifications

- F-005: most tests of the acceptance run call the relayed tool `whoami`, including in their positive controls (T05, T06, T12, T13, T15): without a relay, they were « non jouable ». F-008 brings the relay: no test is « non jouable » for lack of a relay any more.
- The `oauth_checks.py` and `socle_checks.py` checks probe the POC through a **tool** named `ping`; against v1, the adapter has them probe through the MCP **method** `ping` (no tool in F-005, BR-067); the expected statuses (200, 401, 403) do not change. The POC targets stay unchanged.
- Barrier: the adapter computes the TOTP code from the bench's throwaway TOTP secret (BR-010), as it used to enter the authorization secret of the POCs.
- Provisioning by the CLI in the bench container (`docker exec`), failures injected from outside (database file made non-writable or lock held on the throwaway volume; key file removed), tokens forged with the throwaway signing key generated by the bench and mounted as a secret (BR-010): no test route in the image (BR-068).
- The checks that require the relay (BR-013, BR-015/BR-022, BR-016) were « non jouable » against v1 in F-005; since F-008 they are played, BR-015 and BR-022 against the real `gitea-mcp` 1.11.0 on the throwaway Gitea instance.
- 16/16 réussis against v1: acceptance criterion of HULL-9, reached by F-008 (`results-v1.json`: 16 réussis, 0 échoué, 0 non jouable, full pass).
- Readings of the v1 target (post-freeze amendment of AC-29 of F-008, operator decision, 2026-10-31; POC targets unchanged): T06 forges the token type through the `typ` header (BR-054, BR-014), T07 and T08 are judged on B's upstream, which receives nothing (BR-115, BR-113), T09 on the masking of BR-130 (until F-015), T10 on the stability of the tools already listed (BR-109); they are written in `mapping.json` and `results-v1.json`, and will be tightened when HULL-10 provides per-tool masking.
- An `--only` pass is not conclusive (bench README): acceptance reads a full pass.
- F-015 (v1 reading of T09 tightened): T09 is read on a tool hidden by `deny` (BR-174, BR-176), `hidden_admin`, classified `read`, absent from role A's `tools/list` next to `whoami`, the visible sibling on the same connection; its direct calls are refused and, in the window, the upstream of its connection receives no tool call and no entry names `hidden_admin` (the other entries, including the generic-tool bypass variant's call to a visible read of another connection, are recorded outside the verdict). The connection shared with a sensitive role of the previous reading is gone.
- F-015: the bench upstream publishes no annotation (class `destructive` by default, BR-171); the v1 adapter reads each catalogue (`connection tools`), classifies `whoami`, `get_note`, `hidden_admin` as `read`, `write_note`, `flaky` as `write`, and the `gitea` service's `issue_write` as `write` (BR-015 and BR-022 controls), then puts `hidden_admin` in `deny` on the placements of roles A and B.
- F-015 (AC-2 against real `gitea-mcp`): the control `AC-2/F-015` plays the reviewer of F-015 §3.3 on a sensitive role `acme/app/coffre/review` with its own Gitea connection `acme/gr` (never `acme/ga`, whose placement on a sensitive role would make role A's sensitive, BR-186): `pull_request_write` is absent from `tools/list`, a direct merge call answers "unknown tool" with no frontal entry for it, and a COMMENT review through `pull_request_review_write` is served on a pull request the adapter opens; the announced `method` enum holds `create` and `submit` only (BR-179).
