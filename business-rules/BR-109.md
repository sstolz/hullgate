# BR-109 · `tools/list` announces only the tools of the connections placed and active on the URL's role

## Rule

The tool list of a role is the union of the tools announced by the upstream of each of its connections **placed on that role** and **active**, as the action policy exposes them (BR-123, BR-174); the role is the one of the authorised URL, never a parameter of the request; a connection that is not placed, `pending`, `reauth_required`, revoked or removed brings no tool and receives no exchange.

## Clarifications

- A role without connection, or whose upstreams announce nothing, returns an empty list, as in F-005.
- Upstreams sometimes paginate: the relay follows `nextCursor` up to 500 tools per connection and 10 pages; beyond that, the connection is treated as unavailable for this list (BR-111). The client's list is served in one page; a client cursor is ignored.
- The connections of a role are fetched **in parallel**, under the bounds of BR-108 (the token renewal and the list of one connection do not add to those of the others); the client's list has a global deadline of 30 s (H21): a connection not back at the deadline is omitted (BR-111, reason `deadline`).
- Definitions (upstream name aside, BR-110) are relayed unchanged: description, input schema, annotations (H8), except the announced `enum` of a tool carrying an argument rule, narrowed to the allowed values (BR-179).
- A connection placed on a sensitive role reaches the upstream like any other; what it exposes is decided by BR-175 and BR-186.
